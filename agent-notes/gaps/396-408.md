# Pages 396–408 (−2,595,000,000 to −2,530,000,000)
_Dug: 2026-11-23. Status: 12 candidates, 5 added (2 of them just outside the stretch, on p395 and p411)._

## Context
Neoarchean, late Archean. Neighbours: Jeerinah impact (p389, 2.63 Ga), supercratons (p395, "assembled by ~2.6 Ga",
bleeker2003), organic haze span 2.65–2.50 Ga (zerkle2012; prints start/end only), Wittenoom impact (p409,
2.53 ± 0.03 Ga, rasmussen2005), oxygen whiff 2.50 Ga (p414), BIF peak (p415).
Threads through the gap: Hamersley Group BIF deposition (Pilbara, 2.60–2.45 Ga), Campbellrand–Malmani carbonate
platform (Kaapvaal, ~2.59–2.52 Ga), Slave craton granite bloom and cratonization, Zimbabwe craton stabilisation
(Chilimanzi granites → Great Dyke), Carajás (Amazonia) granites and Cu–Au, Dharwar late granites (Closepet 2.51 Ga,
just after), North China ~2.5 Ga event (mostly after the stretch).
Cratons with rock of this age: Pilbara (Hamersley Basin), Kaapvaal (Ghaap/Chuniespoort), Zimbabwe, Slave, Carajás,
Dharwar, North China, Yilgarn (latest granites ~2.62).
