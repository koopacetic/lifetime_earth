_Dug: 2026-11-22. Status: 12 candidates, 3 added._

## Context
Earliest Eoarchean (Archean base 4,031 Ma falls on p108). The terrestrial record is a handful of gneiss
complexes (Acasta, Slave craton; possibly Saglek–Hebron, Labrador; Napier, Antarctica, now revised younger) plus
detrital and xenocrystic zircon (Jack Hills/Narryer, Beartooth). Neighbours: p110 Nuvvuagittuq vent fossils (wide
range), p112 Acasta Gneiss 4.00–4.03 Ga (bowring1999); p131 Nectaris, p134 lunar strong dynamo span, p135 Imbrium
(merle2014 Apollo 14 phosphates 3,938 ± 4 Ma already cited there), p145 Late Heavy Bombardment span (bottke2017,
4.2–3.5 Ga) runs through the whole stretch, as does the lunar dynamo span (4.25–3.56 Ga).
