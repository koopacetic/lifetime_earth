# Pages 556–568 (−1,795,000,000 to −1,730,000,000)
_Dug: 2026-11-23. Status: 13 candidates, 6 added._

## Context
Earliest Statherian (the period starts at 1800 Ma, p555), late in the assembly of Columbia/Nuna (`columbia-nuna` span
ends 1.80 Ga on p541; `columbia-breakup` p630). Neighbours: `sulfidic-ocean` (p547), `oldest-eukaryote-fossils`
(p569, 1.78–1.68 Ga), then the p570–585 dig (agent-notes/gaps/570-585.md: Grand Canyon basement, Broken Hill, Labradorian,
Changcheng, Mt Isa, Mazatzal). No spans ran through the stretch before this dig.
Rock of this age: Laurentia's final stitching (Big Sky / Cheyenne belt, Yavapai arc, Kivalliq/Nueltin post-orogenic
magmatism after the Trans-Hudson), North China (Xiong'er LIP, dykes), Amazonia (Avanavero LIP, Roraima), Baltica and
Sarmatia (TIB-1, Korosten AMCG), Australia (McArthur Basin McDermott and Wollogorang formations, Capricorn orogeny,
early Kimban).
