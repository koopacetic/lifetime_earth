# Pages 436–450 (−2,395,000,000 to −2,320,000,000)
_Dug: 2026-11-23. Status: 16 candidates; 5 added (3 in the stretch, 2 on p435 just before it), rest proposed or rejected._

## Context
- Before: Ongeluk LIP + Makganyene glaciation 2,426 Ma (p429, gumsley2017); ozone shield ~2.4 Ga (p435, goldblatt2006);
  Superia break-up 2.45 Ga (p425); Huronian glaciations span 2.45–2.21 Ga (start p423, end p475; `t` −2.33 Ga).
- After: Great Oxidation 2.32 Ga (p451, bekker2004, luo2016). Pages 452–468 belong to the next-door dig
  (boundary: this dig owns events older than −2,320,000,000).
- Time: Siderian (Paleoproterozoic). The whole window sits in the proposed 2.45–2.2 Ga "tectono-magmatic lull":
  little igneous rock of this age anywhere, which is why the stretch is thin.
- Cratons with rock of this age: Kaapvaal (Postmasburg Group: Hotazel; Duitschland, Rooihoogte), Superior (Huronian),
  Pilbara (Turee Creek Group), Rae (Arrowsmith orogeny), Dharwar (dyke swarms), Lewisian/NW Scotland (Scourie dykes),
  North China (Xiong'ershan), Fennoscandia/Kola (Sumian–Sariolian).

## Searched, nothing usable
- **Huronian (Ramsay Lake, Bruce, Gowganda)**: no direct ages inside the window. Gowganda is older than the 2318 ± 8 Ma
  Gordon Lake tuff and younger than ~2.45 Ga; Kurucz et al. 2021 ("Earth's first snowball event", Precambrian Research
  365, 106408) has no abstract online. The existing `huronian-glaciation` span covers it.
- **Hekpoort / Timeball Hill**: Hekpoort max age 2248 Ma (younger); Timeball Hill 2.31–2.32 Ga (next-door dig, and in `great-oxidation`).
- **Widgiemooltha / Sebanga Poort dykes**: ~2.41 Ga (p433), outside the stretch; not looked up.
- **Hearne / Superior**: no 2.40–2.32 Ga event found (Matachewan 2.45 Ga, Senneterre 2.22 Ga, Biscotasing 2.17 Ga are outside).
  The Rae craton's Arrowsmith orogeny (#2) is the one Canadian-shield event in the window.
- **Kola / Fennoscandia**: 2.3 Ga dolerite dykes in the Kola rift (Doklady 2018, 10.1134/s1028334x18030327) and the
  2.46–2.35 Ga drusite complex of the Belomorian belt: dates too loose or minor.
- **Impacts**: none known between 2.49 Ga (Dales Gorge–Kuruman, p417) and Vredefort (2.02 Ga).
- **Earth–Moon / day length**: no tidal-rhythmite record in the window.
- **GOE timing** (Luo 2016 2.33 Ga; Warke 2020; Poulton 2021; Philippot 2018): all at or outside the stretch's ends; the
  `great-oxidation` entry (p451) already cites luo2016. A separate "When did oxygen arrive?" `debated` entry would
  duplicate it.
- **Life**: apart from #5, nothing datable in the window (Paleoproterozoic microfossil record thin before 2.1 Ga).
