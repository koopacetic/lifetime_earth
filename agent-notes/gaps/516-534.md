_Dug: 2026-11-22. Status: 18 candidates, 7 added (6 in the stretch, 1 next door on p515)._

## Context
Orosirian period, Paleoproterozoic. Before the dig the stretch held only `columbia-nuna` (p525, t of a 2.1–1.8 Ga
span; its start/end entries print outside the stretch, so the pages read as empty). Neighbours: Chang'e-5 basalts
(p509), Vredefort (p510), Sagittarius passage (p535), _Grypania_ and Gunflint-age iron formation 1874 Ma (p540,
schneider2002), Sudbury (p545). Lomagundi ended ~2.06 Ga (karhu1996), so the stretch sits in the post-excursion
"Shunga" carbon-burial interval. Rock of this age: Karelia and Kola (Fennoscandia), the Canadian Shield
(Snowbird, Cape Smith, Wopmay, Taltson–Thelon, early Trans-Hudson), Western Australia (Capricorn Orogen),
North China (Khondalite Belt), Tanzania (Usagaran).

## Searched, nothing usable
- **Impacts**: no crater or spherule layer dated 1.995–1.90 Ga; Vredefort (2.023 Ga) and Sudbury (1.85 Ga) bracket it.
- **Glaciations**: none known between the Huronian/Makganyene (~2.4–2.2 Ga) and the Neoproterozoic.
- **Life**: Gunflint microfossils are 1878 ± 2 Ma (p539, outside; in schneider2002's abstract); _Grypania_ 1874 Ma
  (p540). No well-dated eukaryote or macrofossil inside the stretch; gibson2017 says only "by 1.9–1.4 Ga".
- **Banded iron formation**: the ~1.88 Ga Lake Superior pulse (schneider2002) starts just after the stretch.
- **Moon / sky**: mighani2020 gives the lunar dynamo end only as "between ~1.92 and ~0.80 Ga" (no event). No
  day-length point in range.
- **LIPs and dyke swarms**: no well-dated major LIP pulse with a DOI'd abstract inside the stretch besides the Onega
  lavas (#8); Circum-Superior / Molson (~1.88 Ga) is just after it.
- **Ore deposits**: Pechenga Ni (#4) is the only one found; Thompson Ni belt, Kiruna and Lake Superior iron are ~1.88 Ga.
