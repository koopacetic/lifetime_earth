## Context
- Before (p275): nitrogen fixation from 3.2 Ga (stueken2015). Pages 231–262: Barberton/Pilbara Paleoarchean batch,
  giant impacts boiling the ocean 3.29–3.23 Ga (lowe2015).
- After (p315): first oxygen whiffs (crowe2013), plate tectonics onset ~3.0 Ga (tang2016); p325 oxygenic photosynthesis
  (planavsky2014). Dossier 326-374.md continues from 2.945 Ga.
- Time: Mesoarchean (3.2–2.8 Ga). The Paleo/Mesoarchean boundary at 3.2 Ga sits on page 275.
- Cratons with rock of this age: Pilbara (West Pilbara Superterrane: Whundo, Regal, Cleaverville, Dixon Island),
  Kaapvaal (Dominion Group, ~3.1 Ga Barberton granites, Moodies just before), North Atlantic craton (Ivisaartoq,
  Maniitsoq), Dharwar, Superior (rare).
- Threads through: Vaalbara (zegers1998, ≥3.1 Ga); nitrogen-fixation data 3.2–2.75 Ga (stueken2015);
  "when did plate tectonics start" (this stretch holds most of the evidence for an answer of ~3.1–3.0 Ga).
- Cached abstracts grep: only knauth2003, lowe2015, tang2016, westall2015 and zegers1998 mention these ages; none adds
  a new fact inside the window.
