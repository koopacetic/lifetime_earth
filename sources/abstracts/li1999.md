## Verification (2026-11-23)
- Abstract (via search; secondary index pages): mafic to ultramafic dykes and sills in South China dated 828 ± 7 Ma, identical in age to the 827 ± 6 Ma Gairdner Dyke Swarm in Australia (thought plume-related); with granite emplacement and rapid unroofing over ~1000 km, interpreted as the arrival of a plume head beneath South China; Rodinia breakup may have started with a plume that initiated rifting ~820 Ma. Later work disputes the plume model (arc setting proposed).
