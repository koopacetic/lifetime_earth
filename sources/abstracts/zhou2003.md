## Abstract
(TLDR) A new Ginkgo fossil is described that was collected from the Lower Cretaceous fossil Lagerstätte (the Yixian Formation, which is over 121 Myr old) in China and which fills the gap in the fossil record of more than 100 Myr, indicating that their morphology has changed little for over 100 Myr.
