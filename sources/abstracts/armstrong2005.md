## Verification (2026-11-23)
- Abstract (via search; ANU research portal): magmatic zircons from the Nchanga Granite give an ion-microprobe age of 883 ± 10 Ma, a reliable maximum age for the start of Katanga Supergroup (Roan Group) deposition; Nchanga-age detritus in lower Roan sediments shows sedimentation began only after the granite was exhumed and eroded; rules out correlation with the ~950 Ma Mbuji Mayi Group.
