## Verification (2026-11-22)
- Abstract read on the Macquarie University research portal (researchers.mq.edu.au, publication page; quoting limited there): it describes "a widespread reduction in magmatic activity on Earth beginning about 2.45 Ga and lasting for 200-250 My", based on the global distribution of U/Pb zircon ages from subduction-related granitoids and detrital zircons; few arc greenstones or TTGs, only one LIP in the window, little Nd/Hf evidence of crustal growth, unconformities and a gap in banded iron formation; proposed cause: an episodic mantle regime in which much of the plate circuit stagnates.
- Contested later: active 2.4–2.3 Ga magmatism and orogeny in the Rae craton (Arrowsmith orogeny; neil2025, partin2023) and the southern North China Craton (doi 10.2113/2024/lithosphere_2023_234).
