## Verification (2026-11-23)
Read from the public uncorrected proof (UNSW-hosted PDF of GTS2012 ch. 16). Quotes:
- p. 319: Cloud (1972) "identified the oldest modality as the Hadean ... from the formation of Earth to the age \"of the oldest confidently dated rocks\". Hadean is derived from the Greek word, Hades, the ancient Greek underworld".
- p. 319: "Archean (derived from the ancient Greek word Arkhe, meaning beginning, or origin)".
- p. 319: "Proterozoic (derived from the Greek for 'early life', coined by S.F. Emmons in 1888, according to Windley, 1995)".
- Fig. 16.1 caption: "all divisions of the Precambrian are chronometric except for the base of the Ediacaran, which is chronostratigraphic and marked by a GSSP".
- p. 321: the chronometric scheme "was adopted by the ICS in 1990 (Plumb, 1991)"; boundaries "selected so as to enclose or delimit principal cycles of sedimentation, orogeny, and magmatism, but ... defined in years without specific reference to any bodies of rock" (quoting Plumb, 1991, p. 139).
