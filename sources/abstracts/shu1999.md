## Abstract
(TLDR) The discovery of two distinct types of agnathan from the Lower Cambrian Chengjiang fossil-Lagerstätte imply that the first agnathans may have evolved in the earliest Cambrian, with the chordates arising from more primitive deuterostomes in Ediacaran times (latest Neoproterozoic, ∼555 Myr BP), if not earlier.
