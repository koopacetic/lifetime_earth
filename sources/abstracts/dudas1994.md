## Abstract
Diabase dykes of the Sudbury swarm in the Southern Province have baddeleyite U-Pb ages of 1235 +7/-3 Ma. Deformed metadiabase dykes in the adjacent Grenville Province, formerly correlated on the basis of chemistry, have the same age, and in addition contain metamorphiczircon formed at ~1000Ma, derived from igneous baddeleyite. This late Grenvillian age of metamorphism is corroborated by whole rock-garnet Sm-Nd ages. Sudbury metadiabase cuts earlier fabrics in metasedimentary gneiss in the Grenville Province, associated with metamorphism at ~1445 Ma. The Sudburydykes are interpreted to have intruded across a structural and metamorphic front established at this time during a quiescent period preceding Grenvillian orogeny.
