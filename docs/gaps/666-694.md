_Dug: 2026-11-22. Status: 14 candidates, 10 added (7 in the stretch, 3 just outside)._

## Context
- Before (p655–665): mid-Proterozoic low oxygen (planavsky2014b, span); Mackenzie dyke swarm 1267 Ma (p661);
  early inner-core nucleation estimate (p665).
- After (p695–705): Rodinia assembles (li2008), SW Laurentia plume + Midcontinent Rift flare-up 1097 Ma (mohr2024),
  _Bangiomorpha_ 1.047 Ga (gibson2017), Talakh-Khaya superchron (pavlov2010).
- Time: Mesoproterozoic; Ectasian (1.4–1.2 Ga) → Stenian (1.2–1.0 Ga), boundary at p671.
- Big picture: Nuna/Columbia finishes breaking up (zhao2004: final breakup 1.3–1.2 Ga, Mackenzie and Sudbury dykes)
  and Rodinia starts assembling (Grenvillian–Namaqua–Musgrave belts). The "boring billion" in full swing.
- Rock of this age: Laurentia (Grenville Province, Adirondacks, Sudbury dykes, Midcontinent Rift, Gardar in Greenland),
  Scotland (Stoer Group), Australia (Musgrave, Albany–Fraser, Kimberley/Argyle), Kalahari (Umkondo, Namaqua–Natal,
  Premier kimberlite), West Africa (Taoudeni basin).
