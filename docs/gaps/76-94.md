# Pages 76–94 (−4,195,000,000 to −4,100,000,000)
_Dug: 2026-11-22. Status: 9 candidates, 5 added (4 inside the stretch, 1 next door on p96)._

## Context
Hadean. No rock of this age survives on Earth; the record is detrital and xenocrystic zircon (Jack Hills and
Mt Narryer, Yilgarn Craton; rare grains in China and Canada), plus Moon, Mars and meteorite samples.
Neighbours: p75 earliest geodynamo (tarduno2015, borlina2020) and LUCA (moody2024); p95 possible carbon of
biological origin in a 4.1 Ga zircon (bell2015); p105 Mercury resurfaced (marchi2013); p112 Acasta Gneiss.
Threads through the stretch: bombardment (bottke2017: lunar melt rocks and meteorite shock ages suggest relative
quiescence ~4.0–4.2 to 4.4 Ga, then elevated flux), Mars dynamo (mittelholz2020 vs lillis2008).
