# Pages 736–749 (−895,000,000 to −825,000,000)
_Dug: 2026-11-23. Status: 12 candidates, 6 added (1 just outside, p735)._

## Context
Proterozoic · Neoproterozoic · Tonian. Rodinia is assembled (`rodinia-assembles` span ends at −900 Ma, p735) and its
breakup span (`rodinia-breakup`, li2008) starts at −825 Ma (p750). Neighbours: _Ourasphaira_ fungi (p725),
Copernicus crater (p755). No spans run through the stretch. Rock of this age: Laurentia (Mackenzie Mountains,
Shaler Supergroup, Big Cottonwood), Scotland (Moine), South Australia (Adelaide rift onset), South China (Jiangnan
orogen), Central Africa (Copperbelt basement), West Africa (Manso dykes), Arabian–Nubian Shield (first juvenile arcs).

## Searched, nothing usable
- Impacts: no terrestrial craters or spherule layers known between 895 and 825 Ma; no dated lunar crater in range (Copernicus is 800 Ma).
- Glaciation: none; the first Neoproterozoic glaciations are ~717 Ma (Sturtian); Kaigas ~740 Ma refuted (rooney2015).
- Carbon isotopes: Bitter Springs anomaly is 811–789 Ma (p752–757), outside; the Tonian high-δ13C interval before it is poorly dated (Halverson et al. 2005, 10.1130/B25630.1, has its oldest direct age at 760 Ma).
- Oxygen: no dated oxygenation pulse found inside the window.
- Ore deposits: Copperbelt ores themselves are younger than the stretch (only the basement granite is in range).
- Galaxy/Sun: no dated event; Sagittarius passage at ~1 Ga already in the book.
- Molecular clocks: crown-animal estimates (~833–650 Ma) too broad to place.
- Kimberlites/diamonds, komatiites: none known.
