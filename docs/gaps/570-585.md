_Dug: 2026-11-23. Status: 15 candidates, 6 added._

## Context
Statherian (late Palaeoproterozoic), the heyday of the supercontinent Columbia/Nuna (`columbia-nuna` p525,
`columbia-breakup` p630). Neighbours: `oldest-eukaryote-fossils` (p569, 1.78–1.68 Ga); on p587–588 the McArthur River
ore, protosterol biota, Qingshania and Wiborg rapakivi. No spans ran through the stretch before this dig.
Rock of this age: Laurentia's growing southern margin (Yavapai, Mazatzal provinces, Grand Canyon basement) and eastern
margin (Labradorian), northern Australia (McArthur, Mt Isa, Broken Hill/Curnamona, Gawler Kimban), North China
(Changcheng Group, AMCG suites), Baltica (TIB, early Gothian).
