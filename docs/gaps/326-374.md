  Sinqeni Mo isotopes ≥2.95 Ga, oxygenic photosynthesis (planavsky2014).
- After (p375): fossil raindrops and thin air, Ventersdorp ~2.7 Ga (som2012, som2016); retracted Archean biomarkers.
- Time: late Mesoarchean → Neoarchean (boundary 2.8 Ga, page 355, drawn from timescale.json).
- Cratons with rock of this age: Kaapvaal (Pongola, Witwatersrand, Ventersdorp), Pilbara (Fortescue Group),
  Zimbabwe (Belingwe), Superior (Abitibi, Kenoran), Yilgarn (Kambalda), Wyoming (Tetons).
- Threads through: Vaalbara (zegers1998, 3.1 Ga to ≥2.7 Ga); oxygen whiffs; nitrogen fixation data 3.2–2.75 Ga (stueken2015).
