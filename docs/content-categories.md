## Leads from sources already cited
Dated facts found in the cached abstracts (`sources/abstracts/`) that the book doesn't use yet. The abstract states
each one, so they need no new source. Added 2026-11-21: giant impacts boiling the ocean (lowe2015), the oldest
secure cyanobacteria and eukaryote fossils (rasmussen2008), the phosphorus shift (reinhard2016) and the missing old
craters (mazrouei2019).
- connelly2012: chondrules formed from the time of CAIs until 4,564.71 Ma, over ~3 Myr (page 1, crowded).
- wang2017: the angrite parent body's dynamo started 4–11 Myr after Solar System formation.
- bouvier2018: Mars had a primordial crust by 4,547 Ma that survived ~100 Myr.
- dauphas2011: Moon-forming impact 50–150 Myr after Solar System formation (third Moon-age estimate).
- mojzsis2019: giant-planet migration began ca. 4,480 Ma with a ~30 Myr comet influx; prebiotic niches since ~4,400 Ma.
- wilde2001: Acasta's oldest components are 4,030 Ma (check against the Acasta event's 4,015 ± 15).
- byerly2002: four large impacts in Barberton/Pilbara between 3.5 and 3.2 Ga.
- williams2000: banded iron formation at 2,450 Ma records 14.5 synodic months per year and a Moon at 0.906 of today's
  distance; mean lunar recession 1.24 cm/yr over 2,450–620 Ma (footer points).
- gibson2017: eukaryotes evolved by 1.9–1.4 Ga.
- rooney2015: non-glacial sedimentation 753–717 Ma; a proposed global ~740 Ma Kaigas glaciation is refuted.
- hodych1992: Manicouagan (214 Ma) may have triggered the Carnian/Norian extinction; the Triassic–Jurassic boundary at 202 ± 1 Ma.
- burgess2014: the end-Permian extinction lasted 60 ± 48 kyr; carbon-cycle swings went on for ~500 kyr.
- blackburn2013: CAMP erupted in four pulses over ~600 kyr; dinosaurs then dominated for 136 Myr.
- renne2013: the Chicxulub impact came within 33,000 years of the extinction.
- singer2019: the Brunhes–Matuyama reversal unfolded over ~22 kyr (795–773 ka).
- turgeon2008: magmatism preceded OAE2 by up to ~23 kyr.
- strom1994: Venus's global resurfacing ended abruptly, in under 10 Myr.
- brookfield2020: first trigonotarbid arachnids, with book lungs, 420 Ma; Rhynie Chert 407 Ma (this differs from engel2004's
  396–407; the oldest-insect event uses the latter).
- wallner2016: a supernova within ~100 pc every 2–4 Myr.
- schultz2008: leaf-cutter ants evolved 8–12 Ma (page 913, crowded).
- clark2009: West Antarctic deglaciation began 14–15 ka, with an abrupt sea-level rise ~14.5 ka; Northern Hemisphere deglaciation 19–20 ka.
- vandenbergh2016: hominins may have reached eastern Asia by 2 Ma (hypothesis).
- kempf2023: Saturn's rings exposed ≲100–400 Myr.
- Not yet read: the 13 Archean sources appended by another session (tice2004, tice2006, westall2015, arndt2008, huppert1984/1985,
  knauth2003, hren2009, robert2006, gaucher2008, cheney1996, zegers1998, dekock2009); their abstracts are cached.
